A pack declares the oldest Core it can run on. The Hub compares that number to
the Core actually installed and refuses the combination if it is too old,
rather than failing later at runtime.

## Reading the version numbers

FOS Essentials uses **major . significant . minor**, judged by what the change
costs *you*, not by how large the diff was.

| Position | What it means for your world |
|---|---|
| Major | Rework: something you built has to be redone |
| Significant | The tool gained an ability it did not have |
| Minor | A fix, or an addition you may not even notice |

Read the pack changelog before updating. A prefab you modified never carries
your changes over on its own.
